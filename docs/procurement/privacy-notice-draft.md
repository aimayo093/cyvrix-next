## Proposed section (to add to the privacy policy)

### Business contacts from public tenders and our own research

When a public body publishes a tender, the notice usually names a contact
person with a work email address and telephone number. When we find an
opportunity that fits our services, we keep that published contact on our
record of the organisation that published it. We also keep business contact details that appear in the
opportunities we research or are told about.

- **What we keep:** the person's name, job role if given, work email address
  and work telephone number, the organisation, and where we found them (the
  notice or page).
- **Where it comes from:** public procurement notices on Find a Tender, Public
  Contracts Scotland and Sell2Wales (published under the Open Government
  Licence), and other public business sources.
- **Why, and the lawful basis:** to respond to procurements and business
  opportunities that are relevant to us. Our lawful basis is legitimate
  interests: contacting an organisation about a requirement it has published,
  in the way it asked to be contacted.
- **Who sees it:** our own staff, through our secured administration system.
  It is stored by our hosting and database providers on our behalf. We do not
  sell it or share it for anyone else's marketing.
- **How long we keep it:** for as long as the opportunity is open, then for up
  to two years, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
- **Your rights:** you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or
  delete it, or object to our using it. Use the contact details in this
  policy. We will not send marketing email to an individual without their
  consent.

## AI agents (D4): resolved, no extra wording needed

On 18 November 2026 the founder chose **organisations and roles only** for
agents. Buyer contacts never reach Paperclip or DeepSeek, so there is no
international transfer of this personal data and nothing to add to the
notice for it. The section below is kept only as the record of why.

### Previous open point (superseded)

The founder chose **"allow agent full access"** for D4. Paperclip's agents
run on **DeepSeek, which processes data in China**. If agents are given these
contacts, the notice must also say so, along these lines:

> We use AI software to help us review opportunities. Some of this processing
> takes place outside the UK, in China, through our AI provider. [Transfer
> safeguard: to be confirmed.]

Under UK GDPR a transfer of personal data to China needs a transfer
mechanism, such as the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement or
Addendum, plus a transfer risk assessment. `DECISIONS.md` §15 records that no
such basis exists for DeepSeek today.

**Phase 2 therefore builds no agent access to procurement data.** The text
above stays out of the notice until the founder confirms one of these:
- **(a)** agents see organisations and roles only (no personal data, so no
  transfer and no extra wording);
- **(b)** a transfer mechanism is put in place for DeepSeek, and this wording
  is added;
- **(c)** procurement agents move to a provider with UK or EU processing.
